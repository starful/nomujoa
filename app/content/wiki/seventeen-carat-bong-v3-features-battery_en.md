## 2. History and Context

SEVENTEEN has steadily evolved their light stick design, mirroring their growth as artists. The original Carat Bong (V1) established the core aesthetic: the iconic diamond shape symbolizing Carats (the diamonds) supporting the group. V2 refined this design and improved wireless connectivity. However, the Carat Bong V3, or ‘Nu-Reot Bong,’ launched to high anticipation, marked the biggest leap in functionality.

The V3 model is defined by several key features:
1.  Enhanced Light Output: The LED technology was significantly improved, offering brighter, clearer lighting that ensures visibility even in large venues.
2.  Color Palette: While the official colors of SEVENTEEN are Rose Quartz and Serenity, the V3 offers an extensive color spectrum, capable of displaying almost all 13 member-specific colors and various synchronized moods.
3.  Bluetooth 5.0: This advanced wireless technology ensures more stable and faster pairing with the official SEVENTEEN Light Stick app, crucial for seamless central control during live events.

Powering the Brilliance: Battery Specifications

One of the most frequent questions Carats have concerns the power source. The SEVENTEEN Carat Bong Ver. 3 requires three (3) AAA batteries. For optimal performance, especially during long concerts (which SEVENTEEN is known for), fans are highly advised to use new, high-quality alkaline batteries. Poor-quality or old batteries can lead to connection issues, dimming light, or the light stick failing to respond to central concert control signals. Battery longevity typically ranges between 3 to 6 hours depending on usage intensity and quality, making a fresh set mandatory for each multi-hour event. The battery compartment is conveniently located at the base of the handle, ensuring easy access for replacement.

## 5. FAQ

### Q1: How do I pair my Carat Bong V3 (Nu-Reot Bong) to the concert's central control system?

A: Pairing is typically done through the official SEVENTEEN Light Stick app (available on iOS and Android). Before the concert, download the app, enter your seat information (section, row, number), and follow the in-app instructions to activate the 'Concert Mode.' The light stick usually shifts to a test color and then will be automatically controlled by the venue's RF transmitter once the show begins. Always ensure your Bluetooth is enabled and that you have fresh batteries installed.

### Q2: Can I use rechargeable batteries in my Nu-Reot Bong?

A: While rechargeable batteries (such as NiMH) are environmentally friendly, they often deliver slightly lower voltage (1.2V compared to 1.5V alkaline). For optimal connectivity and brightness, especially crucial for central control during live broadcasts, manufacturers and fans highly recommend using new, high-quality alkaline AAA batteries. Using weak or lower-voltage batteries can interfere with the Bluetooth signal and synchronization capabilities.

### Q3: What should I do if my Carat Bong V3 suddenly stops lighting up or fails to sync during a show?

A: The first step is to check the batteries. If the light stick goes out, immediately swap in a fresh set of AAA batteries (always carry spares!). If the light stick is on but failing to sync, turn it off and back on again, ensuring it is still registered in the official app with the correct seat information. If problems persist, look for the official fan support booths often set up outside the venue; staff members are usually present to diagnose technical issues and offer quick fixes.
