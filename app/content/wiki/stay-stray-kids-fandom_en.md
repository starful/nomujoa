## 1. Introduction

STAY (스테이) is the official fandom name of the South Korean boy group Stray Kids (스트레이 키즈). The name was officially announced on August 1, 2018, approximately six months after the group's official debut on March 25, 2018, under JYP Entertainment. Beyond simply being a title for Stray Kids fans, "STAY" carries a deeper meaning, highlighting the integral role fans play in the group's identity and longevity. It represents the fans as the very reason why Stray Kids are able to "stay" and continue their journey in the music industry.

The announcement of the fandom name was a highly anticipated event for fans, marking a significant milestone in the early stages of Stray Kids' career. It solidified the bond between the group and their supporters, fostering a strong sense of community and belonging. Since its inception, the STAY fandom has grown exponentially, becoming a global force in the K-Pop landscape, contributing significantly to Stray Kids' success and influencing their trajectory.

## 2. Origin and Meaning of the Name

The name "STAY" was carefully chosen to encapsulate the profound connection between Stray Kids and their supporters. Stray Kids themselves were heavily involved in the naming process, demonstrating their commitment to acknowledging the importance of their fans.

The meaning derives from the phrase "Where Stray Kids STAY." Removing the 'r' from "stray" leaves "stay," signifying that the fans are the reason why Stray Kids are able to remain where they are. This cleverly crafted name expresses gratitude and appreciation for the unwavering support, loyalty, and dedication shown by the fanbase. It's not simply a label but a statement of dependency and interconnectedness. It conveys a sense that Stray Kids cannot exist in their current form, or perhaps at all, without the fans providing a place, a reason, and a purpose for them to continue.

Furthermore, the name also resonates with the themes of belonging, acceptance, and finding your place, which are often explored in Stray Kids' music and overall messaging. STAY provides a "home" for Stray Kids, and in return, Stray Kids create music and content that provides solace, inspiration, and community for their STAY. This creates a symbiotic relationship where both artist and fan are mutually enriched.

## 3. History and Evolution of the Fandom

The STAY fandom has grown and evolved significantly since its official establishment in 2018.

*   Pre-Debut and Early Stages (2017-2018): Before their official debut, the group garnered attention through the JYP Entertainment survival show, also titled *Stray Kids*. This show allowed potential fans to connect with the members on a personal level, fostering a strong sense of anticipation and excitement even before the official name "STAY" was announced.
*   Formation of STAY (August 1, 2018): The official announcement of the fandom name marked a pivotal moment. It solidified the bond between the group and their fans and initiated the formal organization and growth of the fandom.
*   Growth and Expansion (2019-2021): As Stray Kids gained popularity with each comeback, the STAY fandom expanded globally. Social media platforms became central hubs for STAY to connect, share their love for Stray Kids, and organize support initiatives. The release of hit songs such as "Miroh," "Side Effects," "God's Menu," and "Back Door" further propelled the group's success and attracted an even wider audience, contributing to the explosive growth of the fandom.
*   Global Recognition and Influence (2021-Present): Stray Kids' increasing international recognition, marked by achievements such as their participation in *Kingdom: Legendary War*, reaching #1 on the Billboard 200 with albums like *ODDINARY*, *MAXIDENT*, and *★★★★★ (5-STAR)*, and selling out world tours, has significantly amplified the STAY fandom's global presence and influence. The fandom actively participates in streaming parties, album buying projects, and social media campaigns to support Stray Kids' activities.

Throughout this period, the STAY fandom has shown incredible resilience and dedication. They have rallied around Stray Kids during challenging times and celebrated their triumphs with unwavering enthusiasm. The evolution of STAY reflects not only the growth of Stray Kids but also the evolving landscape of K-Pop fandoms in general, with increased emphasis on global participation, organized support efforts, and the use of digital platforms to connect and collaborate.
