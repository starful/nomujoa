## 1. Introduction

In the vibrant and competitive landscape of 4th Generation K-pop, few groups have established such a distinct and refreshing identity as STAYC (스타이씨). Comprised of members Sumin, Sieun, Isa, Seeun, Yoon, and J, STAYC debuted under High Up Entertainment in late 2020 and quickly became known for their powerful vocals, sharp choreography, and signature "High-Teen" concept, blending youthful exuberance with confident maturity. As a group’s identity is intrinsically linked to its supporters, the formal announcement of a fandom name is a pivotal moment, cementing the relationship between the artists and their audience.

For STAYC, that name is SWITH (스윗). Far more than just a catchy moniker, SWITH defines the supportive ecosystem that enables STAYC's continued success. Fandom names in K-pop are powerful marketing and cultural tools; they provide fans with a collective identity, foster community, and act as a recognizable brand that reinforces loyalty. Understanding the etymology and cultural weight of SWITH is crucial to grasping the unique relationship between STAYC and its dedicated followers.

## 2. History and Context

The name SWITH was officially announced shortly after STAYC’s groundbreaking debut, marking the beginning of their structured journey alongside their fans. The term SWITH is an ingenious portmanteau, designed to be both meaningful and phonetically pleasing.

Etymologically, SWITH is derived from two components:
1. STAYC: The group's name, which itself is an acronym for "Star To A Young Culture," indicating their aspiration to lead and influence contemporary youth culture.
2. WITH: This addition signifies permanence, togetherness, and unwavering support.

Thus, the core message of SWITH is "Always With STAYC," a promise of perpetual partnership between the artists and their fans throughout their careers.

However, the name also enjoys a secondary, implied meaning. Pronounced similarly to the English word "Sweet," SWITH perfectly complements STAYC’s bright, catchy, and refreshing musical style. While STAYC often presents powerful performances, the overall mood surrounding the group is inherently positive, cheerful, and amiable—qualities encapsulated by the feeling of "sweetness." High Up Entertainment and the members themselves frequently use the term with great affection, confirming its intention to foster a comfortable, supportive, and mutually uplifting environment. This deliberate choice of a name that is both a concrete promise (With) and an emotional descriptor (Sweet) has resonated deeply, establishing SWITH as one of the most beloved fandom names in the current K-pop scene.

## 3. Real-world Examples and Fan Culture

The integration of SWITH into STAYC’s promotional activities and fan interactions is comprehensive and creative. On social media platforms like Weverse, Twitter, and V-Live, the members constantly address their audience directly as "Uri SWITHs" (Our SWITHs), reinforcing the familial bond.

One of the most notable manifestations of the SWITH identity is the official lightstick. Known affectionately as the "Kkukkuk" (꾹꾹이), this lightstick features a unique, colorful design resembling a cute, high-teen toy, perfectly matching the group's aesthetic. During live performances and fan meetings, the sea of Kkukkuk lightsticks symbolizes the unity of SWITH, providing vibrant, coordinated support. Fan chants are another crucial element; during major hits like "ASAP" or "RUN2U," the synchronized shouting of the members' names and the closing declaration of "STAYC LOVE SWITH" creates a powerful, electric atmosphere.

Furthermore, SWITH culture often thrives on meme creation and thematic content that plays off STAYC’s unique personality. For example, the viral success of lines like Yoon's signature "P.O.W!" often finds its way into SWITH fan art and celebratory tweets, proving that the fandom is quick to internalize and amplify the group’s distinct charms. This active participation highlights the fact that SWITHs are seen not just as passive consumers, but as essential creative partners in the "Star To A Young Culture" vision. Their energetic online presence contributes significantly to STAYC's continuous trending status globally.

## 5. FAQ

### Q1: When was the SWITH fandom name officially announced?

A: The SWITH fandom name was officially announced in February 2021, just a few months after STAYC's debut in November 2020. This timing allowed the fandom to quickly organize and rally support under a unified name for their first major comeback cycle.

While the name itself sounds cute and squishy, the overall design and bright color palette were created to reflect STAYC’s distinctive "high-teen" and energetic aesthetic, offering a recognizable and playful symbol for the fandom.
