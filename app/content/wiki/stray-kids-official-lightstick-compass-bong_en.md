## 1. Introduction

In the vibrant ecosystem of K-POP, the official lightstick serves as far more than just a concert accessory; it is a profound symbol of identity, unity, and the unbreakable bond between an artist and their fandom. For JYP Entertainment's powerhouse group Stray Kids, this representation comes in the form of the distinctive "Compass Bong" (나침봉, *Na-chim-bong*). Debuted in 2020, this unique piece of official merchandise immediately captured attention, not only for its sleek design but also for its deeply symbolic connection to the group’s core narrative.

The concept of Stray Kids revolves around members finding their way, breaking conventional boundaries, and charting their own course in the music industry—a theme perfectly encapsulated by the lightstick's primary feature: a navigational compass. This article delves into the design, meaning, technological features, and cultural significance of the Compass Bong, affirming its status as an indispensable item for every STAY (Stray Kids’ fandom name). Understanding this lightstick is understanding a vital piece of the Stray Kids’ philosophy and their ongoing global domination.

## 2. History and Context

The Stray Kids official lightstick was first unveiled in late 2019, with sales commencing in 2020, marking a major milestone in the group’s rapidly ascending career. Unlike the common sphere or logo-based designs prevalent among K-POP lightsticks, the Compass Bong features a transparent dome housing a fully functioning, motorized compass needle that rotates 360 degrees.

Design Philosophy: Direction and Autonomy

The choice of the compass is deliberate and highly contextualized within the group’s discography and conceptual universe. Stray Kids debuted with the theme of being "lost" kids who reject societal norms and seek to forge their own way. Songs like "Miroh" (Maze) and the overall narrative of their early EP series underscored this journey of self-discovery. The Compass Bong symbolizes that while the path may be confusing, the lightstick guides the group (and the fandom) forward, offering a steady, shared direction.

The handle of the lightstick is clean and black, contrasting sharply with the illuminated compass head, which can display a variety of colors, primarily a deep red and white, often coordinating with concert production and the official colors, although Stray Kids has not formally declared a specific official color set like many other groups. Functionally, the lightstick utilizes Bluetooth technology, allowing it to sync wirelessly with concert systems, changing colors and pulsating rhythmically to the music, transforming the venue into a synchronized, moving ocean of light—the signature "Compass Ocean."

## 5. FAQ

### Q1: What is the official name of the Stray Kids lightstick, and why is it called the Compass Bong?

A: The official Korean name is *Na-chim-bong* (나침봉), which literally translates to "Compass Rod" or "Compass Stick." Internationally, it is widely known as the Compass Bong. It received this name due to its unique design feature: a rotational, visible compass needle enclosed in the head, symbolizing Stray Kids' continuous journey and the concept of finding their own path in the vast K-POP landscape.

### Q2: How does the compass needle inside the lightstick work?

A: The needle is not a traditional magnetic compass. It is operated by a small, internal motor controlled via the lightstick’s Bluetooth capabilities. During concert viewing, when synced with the central control system, the needle can spin or remain stationary as part of the programmed lighting effects, adding a kinetic element that differentiates it from static lightsticks.

### Q3: Where is the best place to purchase the official Stray Kids Compass Bong, and what should buyers look out for?

A: The official lightstick should primarily be purchased through official JYP Entertainment channels, such as the JYP Shop website, or authorized K-POP retailers (both online and physical stores) to ensure authenticity. Due to its popularity, many unofficial or counterfeit versions exist. Buyers should verify the hologram sticker and confirm that the lightstick is Bluetooth compatible with the official Stray Kids app for concert synchronization.
