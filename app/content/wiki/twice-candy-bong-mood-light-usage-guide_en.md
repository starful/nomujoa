## 1. Introduction

The official light stick is arguably the most sacred piece of merchandise in the K-pop universe, serving as a powerful, collective symbol of fandom identity. For fans of the global superstar group TWICE, known affectionately as ONCE, that symbol is the vibrant and sweet “Candy Bong.” However, the Candy Bong transcends its primary function as a cheering tool. JYP Entertainment recognized the light stick’s beautiful design and integrated a highly sought-after secondary function: the Candy Bong Mood Light.

This dual functionality allows fans to retain the emotional connection of the concert experience and integrate it into their daily environment. The mood light feature transforms the light stick from a temporary event tool into a permanent piece of home decor, symbolizing the perpetual bond between the nine members of TWICE and their devoted fan base. Mastering the use of the Candy Bong Mood Light is not merely about pressing buttons; it's about harnessing the warmth and energy of TWICE’s vibrant concept to create a personalized, comforting space.

## 2. History and Context

The Candy Bong, introduced early in TWICE’s career, derives its name and aesthetic from the group’s bright, bubbly image—specifically referencing the iconic lollipop design often seen in their visual content and the group's official colors: Apricot and Neon Magenta. This unique color pairing symbolizes TWICE’s youthful energy and charm.

The evolution of the Candy Bong has been meticulous. While the original version (v1) was a standard light stick, subsequent iterations, especially the Candy Bong Z (v2) and later the specific Candy Bong Mood Light stand accessories, emphasized practical home use.

How the Mood Light Feature Works:

The transition from light stick to mood light is generally achieved through a specialized docking station or by utilizing the light stick's lower power settings. Unlike standard light sticks that require rapid flashing and high brightness for concert sync, the Mood Light feature utilizes a soft, sustained glow, designed to be easy on the eyes.

*   Dedicated Stand/Cradle: Many versions come with a specialized cradle, often powered by USB, which simultaneously charges the device (if applicable) and activates the softer mood light setting. When the Candy Bong is placed into this cradle, it often defaults to a continuous, calming color rotation or a static color selection chosen by the user.
*   App Integration: The most advanced versions of the Candy Bong (such as the Candy Bong Z) can connect via Bluetooth to the official JYP fan app. This allows ONCE to customize the mood light colors remotely, set timers, or even match the light color to a specific TWICE album concept, such as the deep blue of *Formula of Love: O+T=<3* or the pastel hues of *Taste of Love*.

This innovation solidified the Candy Bong's status not just as merch, but as a practical, high-quality electronic accessory, setting a high bar for K-pop light stick functionality.

## 5. FAQ

### Q1: How do I switch my Candy Bong to the dedicated Mood Light Mode?

The method depends heavily on the specific version of the Candy Bong. For the Candy Bong Z (version 2), the light stick must typically be inserted into the specialized charging/display stand (often sold separately). Once docked and powered via USB, the light stick should automatically enter a low-power, continuous glowing mode, sometimes requiring a quick double-tap of the power button to initiate the color cycling function specifically designed for ambient lighting. Always check the instruction manual for the precise sequence, as mispressing the button can sometimes trigger the high-power concert mode.

### Q2: Can I control the Mood Light colors without manually touching the stick?

Yes, for modern versions like the Candy Bong Z, color control is managed primarily through the official JYP/TWICE mobile application via Bluetooth connectivity. Once paired, you can access a color wheel within the app to select static colors or program custom fading sequences, allowing you to tailor the ambiance perfectly to your mood or setting. This remote control capability is crucial for fans who have their light stick placed high up or in a display case.

### Q3: Why is my Candy Bong not entering Mood Light Mode when placed in the stand?

The most common issues are power or alignment problems. First, ensure the dedicated mood light stand (if you are using one) is correctly plugged into a functional power source (USB outlet or adapter). Second, confirm the connection points (prongs or sensors at the base of the light stick) are clean and properly aligned with the cradle’s contact points. If the light stick's internal batteries are critically low, some models require a brief charge period before allowing access to the continuous, low-power mood light functions. If all else fails, a simple reboot (removing and reinserting batteries, then re-docking) often resolves minor software glitches.
