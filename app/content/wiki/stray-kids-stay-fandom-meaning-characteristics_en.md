## 1. Introduction

In the vibrant, fast-moving landscape of K-POP, the relationship between an artist and their fans is often encapsulated in a singular, meaningful name. For the global phenomenon Stray Kids (SKZ), the dedicated fanbase is known simply as STAY. More than just an identifier, "STAY" is a declaration of commitment, a concept deeply woven into the identity of the group, which has successfully positioned itself as a self-producing powerhouse and a definitive leader of the fourth generation of K-POP.

Stray Kids, composed of members Bang Chan, Lee Know, Changbin, Hyunjin, Han, Felix, Seungmin, and I.N., are internationally celebrated for their raw, genre-bending sound—often spearheaded by their production subunit 3RACHA. While their music speaks of struggling against societal norms and finding one's path, the fandom name, STAY, provides the emotional anchor for this journey. Understanding the etymology and culture of STAY is crucial to grasping the profound impact Stray Kids has had on modern K-POP. It speaks directly to the group’s desire to establish a permanent, welcoming home for those who support them.

## 2. History and Context

Stray Kids officially announced their fandom name, STAY, on August 1, 2018, several months after their official debut in March of the same year. The reveal was highly anticipated, as the name needed to perfectly capture the essence of the group's chaotic yet deeply familial concept.

The brilliance of the name lies in its ingenious wordplay rooted in the group’s original name. The name "Stray Kids" can be broken down to symbolize the group wandering and searching for direction. The name STAY is derived by removing the "R" from the word "STRAY." This "R" signifies "Reason" or "Road." By removing the idea of constantly searching or straying, the fans become the fixed point—the reason the group remains. They are the safe harbor.

The members often reiterate the core message: "You Make Stray Kids STAY." This phrase emphasizes reciprocity; the fans provide stability and a sense of belonging to the eight members who often depict themselves as outsiders navigating a complex world. Unlike fandom names that sometimes reference group lore or specific objects, STAY is direct, simple, and emotionally resonant, highlighting commitment and partnership. It serves as a constant reminder that the group’s intense work ethic and creative output are driven by the persistent support of their fans, establishing a permanent familial bond beyond the typical idol-fan dynamic.

## 3. Real-world Examples and Fan Culture

The culture of STAY is defined by intense loyalty, efficiency in coordinated projects, and a highly protective, yet often humorous, digital presence.

One of the most defining characteristics of the STAY fandom is its organizational strength in global voting and streaming campaigns. With Stray Kids emphasizing self-production and performance, STAYs often focus on breaking records and securing wins (especially on international charts and music shows) as validation of the group’s artistic merit. Their coordinated efforts have been instrumental in pushing SKZ to become the first JYP boy group to consistently hit the top spot on the Billboard 200 chart.

Furthermore, STAYs are famous for their endearing, meme-heavy communication style. Stray Kids members, particularly Bang Chan through his weekly "Chan's Room" V Live broadcasts, maintain constant, casual communication with their fanbase, fostering a sense of familiarity. This closeness allows for the creation of unique, internalized memes and jokes that strengthen community bonds. When a new comeback or award season approaches, the fandom rallies under hashtags that often blend their name with the theme, such as #SKZ_DOMINATION or #STAY_WITH_SKZ.

Crucially, the protective nature of the fandom stands out. Because Stray Kids often share candidly about their personal struggles and intense schedules, STAYs are quick to mobilize in defense of the members, particularly regarding mental health and online harassment. This mirrors the group’s thematic focus on overcoming adversity, turning the fandom into a vigilant, supportive force dedicated to ensuring the members feel safe and appreciated in their creative "home."

## 5. FAQ

### Q1: What does the "R" stand for in the wordplay of Stray Kids/STAY?

A: The "R" that is removed from STRAY to form STAY is often interpreted to stand for "Reason" or "Road." By removing the "R," it implies that the fans (STAY) are the fixed reason and destination for the group, meaning Stray Kids is no longer lost or straying.

### Q2: Is there an official color associated with the Stray Kids fandom, STAY?

A: As of late 2023, JYP Entertainment has not designated an official Pantone color for Stray Kids. However, the fandom often uses a gradient of vibrant, electric colors—particularly shades of red, purple, and blue—in their official light sticks and merchandise, reflecting the energetic and versatile nature of the group's music.

### Q3: When and how often do the Stray Kids members communicate directly with STAY?

A: Stray Kids members are known for their frequent and personal communication. Leader Bang Chan famously hosts a near-weekly segment called "Chan's Room," a casual livestream dedicated to chatting, answering questions, and playing music. Other members use platforms like Bubble (a subscription messaging service) and JYP Fan’s frequently, ensuring a consistently high level of accessibility and personal connection with the fanbase.
