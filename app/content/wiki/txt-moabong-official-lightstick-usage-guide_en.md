## 1. Introduction

In the vibrant ecosystem of K-Pop, the official light stick stands as the most critical physical manifestation of a fandom's identity and loyalty. For TOMORROW X TOGETHER (TXT), the fifth-generation powerhouse from Big Hit Music, this indispensable item is known affectionately as the 'MOA Bong.' The name derives directly from their fandom, MOA (Moment of Alwaysness), symbolizing the perpetual connection between the group and their fans.

The design of the MOA Bong is sleek and meaningful, featuring a clear globe containing an inner mechanism that resembles a magical, futuristic compass, often highlighting the group's signature blue and yellow color palette. The light stick is not merely a flashlight; it is an intelligent, Bluetooth-enabled device that integrates seamlessly into the overall production of TXT’s concerts and fan meetings. For new MOAs or those preparing for their first TXT concert, mastering the use of the MOA Bong is the first step toward becoming a fully integrated member of the synchronous "TXT Ocean," making this usage guide an essential resource.

## 2. History and Context

TXT officially debuted in 2019, but the MOA Bong was unveiled later, building anticipation among the rapidly growing global fandom. Its release was synchronized with the growing need for personalized fan interaction at large-scale venues. Unlike older generations of light sticks, the MOA Bong is designed to function primarily under a Central Control Mode (CCM), a technology that allows concert producers to wirelessly manipulate every light stick in the arena simultaneously.

This technology transforms thousands of individual lights into one unified, pulsating light show, changing colors and patterns according to the performance, choreography, and mood of each song—from the ethereal blue glow accompanying "Blue Hour" to the fiery intensity required for "Good Boy Gone Bad."

### Essential Usage Steps for Concert Mode:

1.  Power: The MOA Bong requires three fresh AAA batteries. Always use brand new, high-quality batteries before a concert to ensure longevity and stable power supply, as the Central Control Mode drains power quickly.
2.  App Pairing: Before arriving at the venue, fans must download the official TXT Light Stick app or utilize the pairing function within the Weverse Shop app.
3.  Registration: Navigate to the Concert Mode pairing section. Fans must input their official ticket information, including the specific seat section, row, and seat number. This step links the light stick’s unique identification chip to its exact physical location in the venue.
4.  Sync Activation: Once registered, the light stick is ready. Upon entering the venue and turning the stick on, the venue’s central system will automatically take over control, ensuring that the light stick flashes the correct color at the correct time, creating the famous synchronized "MOA Ocean."

For instance, during the iconic bridge in songs like "Crown," the MOA Bong will illuminate in a pattern specific to that moment, signifying the unity between the group's narrative and the fans’ shared experience.

Moreover, the light stick features a "Bluetooth Mode" which allows fans to customize the colors outside of a concert environment, giving MOAs the freedom to match their stick’s lighting to their favorite member’s representative color or the concept of a new album release. This customization transforms the MOA Bong into a personal, interactive piece of memorabilia, often used during online viewing parties, V Live sessions, or while creating fan-made content. The act of customizing and displaying the light stick itself is a common ritual, reinforcing the "Moment of Alwaysness" promised by the fandom's name.

## 5. FAQ

### Q1: How do I ensure my MOA Bong works during Central Control Mode (CCM)?

A: The most critical step is successful registration and pairing via the official app (Weverse Shop or TXT Light Stick App) using your exact concert seat details *before* you enter the venue. Always test your light stick connection before leaving home, and replace old batteries with new AAA batteries, as low power can cause intermittent connection issues with the central control system.

### Q2: Can I still use the MOA Bong if I don't pair it to my seat?

A: Yes, you can still use the MOA Bong in "General Mode" by pressing the power button. However, in General Mode, the stick will cycle through basic colors or maintain a single color, and it will *not* participate in the synchronized color changes dictated by the concert production team. For the full experience, pairing is mandatory.

### Q3: Why does my light stick not flash the same colors as others in my section?

A: If your MOA Bong is paired but not syncing correctly, first check your battery strength. If batteries are confirmed strong, confirm that your seat registration details (section, row, seat number) were entered precisely as they appear on your official ticket. A small error in input can lead to the system not recognizing its exact location in the grid, causing visual misalignment. If the issue persists, seek assistance from official staff at the venue's light stick service booth.
