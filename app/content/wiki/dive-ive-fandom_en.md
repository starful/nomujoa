## 1. Introduction

DIVE (다이브) is the official fandom name for the South Korean girl group IVE (아이브), managed by Starship Entertainment. The name was officially announced on December 8, 2021, via IVE's official social media channels, shortly after the group's debut with the single "ELEVEN" on December 1, 2021.  The selection of "DIVE" represents the profound and intimate relationship between IVE and their fanbase, encapsulating themes of support, encouragement, and mutual exploration.  It goes beyond a simple label, signifying a collective journey and shared experience between the artists and their followers.

## 2. Meaning and Symbolism

The name "DIVE" carries significant symbolic weight within the context of IVE and their artistic direction.

*   Deep Connection:  The act of "diving" implies a commitment to exploring the depths, suggesting that DIVEs are dedicated to understanding and supporting IVE beyond surface-level appreciation.  They are willing to delve deeper into the group's music, concepts, and personalities.
*   Unwavering Support:  DIVE represents a sense of security and trust. The fandom serves as a supportive base for IVE, allowing the group to confidently "dive" into new challenges and creative endeavors knowing they have the backing of their fans.
*   Mutual Exploration:  The name suggests a shared journey of discovery.  IVE and DIVE embark on adventures together, with IVE constantly pushing boundaries and DIVEs eagerly embracing and interpreting the group's artistic evolution.  It’s a reciprocal relationship of influence and inspiration.
*   Symbolic Link to IVE's Identity: IVE, as a group name, itself is a shortened version of "I HAVE," emphasizing the group's confidence and presentation of themselves as possessing everything they need. "DIVE" complements this by suggesting fans are diving into the complete package that IVE offers.

## 3. Announcement and Fan Reception

The announcement of the fandom name was highly anticipated by fans following IVE's initial debut success. The unveiling on December 8, 2021, was met with overwhelmingly positive reactions.  Fans expressed excitement and embraced the name, quickly adopting it across social media platforms and fan communities.  The name resonated well with its symbolic meaning and its alignment with IVE's established image. The official IVE fan cafe and social media channels saw a significant increase in activity following the announcement, demonstrating the fandom's strong engagement.
