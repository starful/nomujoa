## 1. Introduction

In the vibrant landscape of fourth-generation K-pop, few groups define self-confidence and unique identity as clearly as JYP Entertainment’s sensational quintet, ITZY. Since their explosive debut in 2019, ITZY has consistently championed the message of self-love and breaking conventional molds. Central to this narrative of conviction and success is their dedicated global fanbase, officially known as MIDZY.

The pairing of “ITZY” and “MIDZY” is not accidental; it is a masterclass in linguistic synergy designed to solidify a reciprocal relationship between the artists and their supporters. Understanding the origin of these names—rooted deeply in the Korean language—is essential to grasping the core philosophy that drives the group’s immense popularity. ITZY (있지, *itji*) can be interpreted as "We have it," signifying the group’s confidence and talent, while MIDZY (믿지, *mitji*) means "You believe (in us)" or "Trust." This dynamic confirms that ITZY’s ability to "have it all" is directly enabled by the unwavering trust and belief of their fans, creating one of the most cohesive and meaningful fandom names in contemporary K-pop culture.

## 2. History and Context

ITZY debuted on February 12, 2019, with the empowering single "DALLA DALLA," immediately setting the tone for their brand—fierce individuality and unshakeable self-acceptance. However, the official designation for their fanbase came several months later.

MIDZY was formally announced on July 8, 2019, a strategic move by JYP Entertainment to give their rapidly growing global community a strong identity. The name MIDZY is derived from the Korean verb *mitda* (믿다), meaning "to believe" or "to trust." When conjugated as *mitji* (믿지), it functions as an affirmation or a question directed at the fans: "You believe, right?" or simply "Believe!"

This linguistic choice is a perfect complement to ITZY’s name, which stems from the Korean verb *itta* (있다), meaning "to have" or "to be there." The implied sentence structure running through this relationship is powerful: "We (ITZY) have everything needed for success, and you (MIDZY) believe in our vision and capability." This mutual affirmation of trust acts as the bedrock of their collective identity. Unlike many fandom names that are acronyms or portmanteaus, MIDZY is a direct declaration of relationship status, signifying loyalty and reliance, mirroring ITZY’s own powerful stage presence built on faith in their own unique style. This foundation of trust is crucial in the high-stakes, competitive environment of K-pop, where group longevity often hinges on fan dedication.

## 5. FAQ

A: MIDZY was officially named by ITZY and JYP Entertainment on July 8, 2019, approximately five months after the group’s official debut in February 2019.

### Q2: What is the direct translation of ITZY and MIDZY from Korean?
A: ITZY (있지, *itji*) is derived from the verb *itta* (있다, to have/to be there), implying "We have it" (referring to talent and success). MIDZY (믿지, *mitji*) is derived from the verb *mitda* (믿다, to trust/to believe), meaning "You believe (in us)."

### Q3: How does the name MIDZY reflect ITZY’s music concept?
