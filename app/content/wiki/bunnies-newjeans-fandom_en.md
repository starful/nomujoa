## 1. Introduction

Bunnies (Korean: 버니즈; RR: *Beonijeu*) is the official fandom name for the South Korean girl group NewJeans (Korean: 뉴진스), managed by ADOR (All Doors One Room), a subsidiary of HYBE Corporation. The name was officially announced on August 6, 2022, via a live broadcast on the NewJeans official YouTube channel. "Bunnies" represents the collective body of fans who support NewJeans and are deeply connected to their music, performances, and overall artistic vision. The name aims to foster a sense of community and belonging among fans, reinforcing their importance to the group’s success and journey.

## 2. Etymology and Meaning

The name "Bunnies" holds multifaceted meanings, reflecting NewJeans' branding and relationship with their fans.

*   Cute and Affectionate: "Bunny" is often associated with cuteness, innocence, and gentle affection. This aligns with NewJeans’ youthful and fresh image, which has been a key component of their appeal since their debut. The term encapsulates the warmth and endearment the group feels towards their fans.
*   Homophony with “Bunny-Heads”: The term "Bunnies" also alludes to the group's name, NewJeans. In Korean, the pronunciation of "Bunnies" (*Beonijeu*) bears a phonetic resemblance to *“ban-jeon”* (반전), which can mean a twist, surprise, or unexpected change. This plays into the playful and innovative nature of NewJeans, signaling that their music and performances often have unexpected elements or twists. It can also playfully imply that NewJeans are like "bunny-heads" for their fans.
*   Represents Support and Loyalty: The imagery of bunnies is often linked to vitality, community, and care. "Bunnies" therefore represents the collective support and unwavering loyalty that fans provide to NewJeans. Just as bunnies are often seen as a group, the fandom acts as a unified force behind NewJeans' success.
*   Part of NewJeans’ Overall Aesthetic: The choice of "Bunnies" fits seamlessly into NewJeans' overall aesthetic, which blends Y2K influences with modern sensibilities. Bunnies can be linked to nostalgic cartoon imagery and playful accessories from the late 90s and early 2000s, reinforcing the group's distinctive brand identity.

## 3. Announcement and Reception

The announcement of the fandom name was a highly anticipated event within the K-pop community. NewJeans, having already made a significant impact with their pre-debut promotion and instant hit songs, created a sense of excitement among potential fans. The official announcement on August 6, 2022, was broadcast live on their YouTube channel, generating substantial buzz on social media platforms.

*   Positive Initial Response: The reception to the name "Bunnies" was largely positive. Many fans embraced the name for its cuteness, relatability, and connection to the group's image. The playful nature of the name and its phonetic link to the group's identity were also well-received.
*   Community Building: Following the announcement, "Bunnies" quickly became a rallying cry for fans. Online communities and social media groups dedicated to NewJeans adopted the name, using it as a symbol of their shared passion and support.
*   Merchandise and Branding: ADOR and NewJeans have since incorporated "Bunnies" into various aspects of their branding. Official merchandise featuring bunny motifs and imagery has been released, further solidifying the name's presence in the fandom. This included lightsticks, plushies, and apparel.

## 4. Fandom Culture and Activities

As "Bunnies," NewJeans fans actively engage in various activities to support the group:

*   Streaming and Charting: Fans actively stream NewJeans' music videos and songs on various platforms to boost their chart performance. Collaborative efforts are often organized to achieve specific streaming goals and break records.
*   Social Media Promotion: Bunnies use social media platforms to promote NewJeans' releases, share updates, and create fan-made content. This includes artwork, edits, translations, and more. Hashtags related to NewJeans and "Bunnies" are frequently trending topics.
*   Physical Album Sales: Participating in mass-buying campaigns and purchasing multiple copies of NewJeans' physical albums to support their album sales and rankings is a significant activity. This often involves coordinating with other fans both domestically and internationally.
*   Voting and Award Shows: Actively participating in voting for NewJeans in award shows and music programs. The fandom mobilizes to ensure that NewJeans receives the recognition they deserve.
*   Fan Projects and Events: Organizing and participating in fan projects, such as anniversary celebrations, birthday greetings, and charity drives in the name of NewJeans. This fosters a sense of community and shared purpose among fans.
*   Concerts and Fan Meetings: Attending NewJeans' concerts and fan meetings to show their support and engage directly with the group. This includes preparing fan chants, banners, and gifts.

## 5. Lightstick and Official Merchandise

ADOR released the official NewJeans lightstick, which also integrates the "Bunny" theme, further cementing its importance to the group's identity. The lightstick, shaped like a bunny head, has become a highly sought-after item among fans. The lightstick’s design and features directly reinforce the fandom’s identity. Numerous other merchandise items also feature bunny motifs, including plushies, keychains, clothing, and accessories, enabling fans to express their affiliation with the fandom in diverse ways.

## 6. Significance within K-Pop Culture

The fandom name "Bunnies" has become a recognizable symbol within the broader K-pop landscape.

*   Symbol of NewJeans' Success: The dedication and support of the "Bunnies" have been instrumental in NewJeans' rapid rise to fame. Their accomplishments are often attributed to the unwavering efforts of their fans.
*   Influence on Fandom Naming Trends: While not a direct origin point, the creative and endearing nature of "Bunnies" contributes to the trend of K-pop fandom names being more personalized and reflective of the group's identity.
*   Positive Fan-Group Dynamic: "Bunnies" is often cited as an example of a well-organized and dedicated fandom, contributing to NewJeans' positive image within the industry.
