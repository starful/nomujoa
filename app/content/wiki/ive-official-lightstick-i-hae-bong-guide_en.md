## 1. Introduction

The official lightstick is more than just a piece of merchandise; it is the physical manifestation of a fandom's identity, symbolizing the bond between the artist and their supporters. For DIVEs (IVE’s dedicated fanbase), this critical accessory is the “I Hae Bong” (아이해봉). Launched to complement IVE’s elegant, polished, and high-fashion concept, the I Hae Bong is a sleek, minimalist device that perfectly encapsulates the group’s visual identity. It is essential equipment for any DIVE attending a concert, fan meeting, or official event, as it allows fans to participate in synchronized light waves and truly become part of the performance’s visual landscape. Given its high demand and importance, knowing the authorized purchasing channels and usage instructions is paramount for new and veteran DIVEs alike.

## 2. History and Context

IVE debuted in late 2021 and rapidly ascended to global stardom based on their sophisticated sound and undeniable stage presence. While many groups release their lightsticks shortly after debut, IVE took their time, ensuring the final design was perfect. The I Hae Bong was officially unveiled in mid-2022, marking a major milestone for the burgeoning fandom.

The name "I Hae Bong" itself is a delightful play on words, combining "IVE" with the general Korean term for lightstick, *eungwonbong*. Its design diverges from the often overly complex structures seen in K-Pop, favoring a clean, futuristic aesthetic. The handle is slender and white, leading up to a clear, crystalline head often interpreted as a stylized star or diamond—a nod to IVE’s luxurious and shimmering stage concept.

Crucially, the I Hae Bong operates using advanced Bluetooth technology. This allows the centralized control system at concert venues to remotely change the lightstick's color and pattern. During a live show, every I Hae Bong transforms into a pixel on a massive, unified screen, creating breathtaking visuals that are perfectly synchronized to the music. This function is why purchasing the official, verified lightstick—rather than a knockoff—is mandatory for full concert participation.

## 3. Real-world Examples and Fan Culture (The Purchase Guide)

Securing an official I Hae Bong requires navigating the specific purchasing ecosystem set up by Starship Entertainment. As with all high-demand K-Pop merchandise, stock can be limited, especially around major tours or comeback periods.

### Official Purchasing Channels:
The most reliable and recommended places to purchase the I Hae Bong are:
1. Starship Square: This is Starship Entertainment’s official online merchandise store, often accessible through platforms like Apple Music or specialized K-Pop retailer hubs. This guarantees authenticity and ensures the product is shipped directly from the source.
2. Official Global Retailers: Verified K-Pop merchandise distributors (such as Weverse Shop, Ktown4u, or dedicated local distributors recognized by Starship) often carry the lightstick. Always check the retailer's official status to avoid counterfeit goods.
3. Venue Sales: For those attending concerts, lightsticks are almost always sold at the venue on the day of the event. However, lines are long, and stock can sell out quickly, making pre-ordering strongly advised.

### Avoiding Counterfeits and Fan Usage:
Purchasing counterfeit lightsticks is a risk, primarily because they lack the necessary internal hardware to communicate with the central control hub (known as the ‘Central Pairing System’). If your lightstick cannot pair via the official IVE lightstick app, it will not participate in the synchronized lighting effects at the concert, significantly diminishing the immersive fan experience. The official lightstick connects to the app via Bluetooth, allowing DIVEs to input their seat number prior to the show, preparing their device for the mass synchronicity.

On social media, DIVEs often use the I Hae Bong in photo challenges, creating elaborate aesthetic displays to showcase their dedication. The lightstick is frequently featured in fan photos taken at home or while traveling, serving as an unofficial flag for the IVE fandom globally.

## 5. FAQ

### Q1: How do I connect my I Hae Bong to the Central Pairing System at a concert?
You must first download the official IVE Lightstick App (usually available on iOS and Android stores). Before the concert begins, open the app, connect your lightstick via Bluetooth, and then use the app interface to input your specific concert date, venue, and seat number. Once confirmed, the lightstick will be automatically controlled by the venue's system. Ensure you have fresh AAA batteries installed before arriving.

### Q2: Is there a specific official color for IVE?
IVE’s official colors are not strictly defined by a single shade, but rather a sophisticated visual palette focusing on various tones of blue, pink, and white/silver, reflecting their ethereal and premium image. The I Hae Bong is capable of displaying a wide spectrum of colors, though often, the venue will utilize icy blue and deep pink shades during synchronized moments.

### Q3: What should I do if my lightstick fails to sync at the venue?
First, ensure you have input the correct seat information into the official app and that Bluetooth is enabled. If problems persist, look for the designated "Lightstick Pairing Booth" or "Merch Help Desk" at the venue. Staff are equipped with diagnostics tools and replacement batteries and can usually troubleshoot connectivity issues before the show starts. Always arrive early to handle technical checks.
