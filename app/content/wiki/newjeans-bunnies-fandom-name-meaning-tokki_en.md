## 1. Introduction

In the hyper-competitive world of K-pop, the relationship between an artist and their fans is cemented through shared identity, often symbolized by a carefully chosen fandom name. For the powerhouse rookie girl group NewJeans—managed by ADOR, a subsidiary of HYBE—that identity is encapsulated by the charming and highly recognizable name, Bunnies (버니즈).

NewJeans exploded onto the global stage in mid-2022, immediately capturing attention with their innovative, analog aesthetic and fresh, minimalist music style. Their success transcended typical rookie expectations, but their unique branding went beyond just music. Central to their visual identity, from early teasers to merchandise, was the distinct silhouette of a rabbit. When the official fandom name, Bunnies, was announced on October 29, 2022, it was not merely a cute choice; it was a deliberate, layered decision that perfectly integrated the fans into the narrative and mythology of the NewJeans universe. Understanding the meaning of 'Bunnies' is key to appreciating the deep, symbiotic connection between the five members and their global fanbase.

## 2. History and Context

The selection of 'Bunnies' is intricately linked to the core conceptual pillars of NewJeans’ brand identity, emphasizing continuity, friendship, and playful innocence.

### The Rabbit Motif: A Central Symbol

From their very first visual assets, the rabbit has been the unofficial mascot and logo of NewJeans. The iconic ‘NJ’ logo is often stylized within a subtle rabbit shape. In Korean, the word for rabbit is *Tokki* (토끼). While the official English name is 'Bunnies,' fans often use the endearing term 'Tokki' when referring to themselves, bridging the gap between the official English branding and the vernacular Korean term.

### The Significance of ‘Bunnies’ (버니즈)

The fandom name serves multiple strategic purposes:

1.  Visual Continuity: It directly corresponds to the group’s visual branding, ensuring that every piece of merchandise, from the album design to the official lightstick (known as the 'Binky Bong'), reinforces the same sweet, youthful image. The Binky Bong, in particular, features a stylized bunny head, making it an essential visual marker for Bunnies at concerts and events.
2.  Double Meaning and Pronunciation: The official name 'Bunnies' is often written in Korean as '버니즈' (Beonijeu). However, ADOR also released a separate app logo for fans that featured the word 'Tokki' clearly incorporated, indicating that both terms are valid and understood ways for fans to self-identify.
3.  Reflecting Youth and Energy: Rabbits are universally associated with curiosity, quickness, and cuteness—traits that align perfectly with the youthful energy and trendsetting nature NewJeans brought to K-pop. The name implies a soft, yet highly energetic, supportive presence.
4.  The Mutual Relationship: The name suggests that Bunnies are the playful, constant companions of NewJeans, hopping alongside the members on their journey. It promotes a feeling of closeness and casual intimacy, which is further emphasized by the group’s unique fan communication platform, Phoning.

This careful selection ensures that the fandom name is not static but actively participates in the lore of the group, functioning as a continuous visual shorthand for the NewJeans concept.

## 3. Real-world Examples and Fan Culture

The identity of a Bunny permeates fan interaction both online and offline, creating specific cultural touchstones unique to the NewJeans community.

### The Binky Bong and Concert Culture

The most tangible manifestation of the Bunny identity is the Binky Bong. Unlike typical lightsticks that often feature complex designs or logos, the Binky Bong is clean, sleek, and immediately recognizable as a glowing rabbit head. At live performances, thousands of Binky Bongs create a collective ‘field of rabbits,’ turning the concert venue into a unified visual representation of the fandom. Fans frequently customize their Binky Bongs with ribbons or stickers, adding a personal touch while maintaining the overall rabbit theme.

### Digital Identity and Communication

On social media platforms like Twitter, Instagram, and TikTok, Bunnies often incorporate the rabbit emoji (🐰) into their usernames, bios, and comments when supporting the group. This simple emoji acts as an instant identifier.

Furthermore, NewJeans utilizes the custom fan communication app, Phoning, which is designed to mimic a late-1990s/early 2000s analog phone interface. This platform encourages casual, almost conversational interaction between the members and their fans, reinforcing the notion that Bunnies are trusted friends rather than distant observers. The playful, unfiltered nature of the Phoning content aligns perfectly with the youthful and approachable image embodied by the 'Bunnies' name.

### Fan Chants and Catchphrases

During music show performances, the official fan chants often integrate the name, creating a powerful collective voice. The simplicity and high recognition factor of the rabbit symbol have also made it a popular meme generator, with fans sharing edits of the members wearing virtual rabbit ears or interacting with famous animated rabbits. This collective, playful use of the symbol solidifies the Bunnies identity as fun, dedicated, and deeply integrated into the NewJeans aesthetic.

## 5. FAQ

### Q1: Is the official fandom name 'Bunnies' or 'Tokki'?

The official English name designated by ADOR is 'Bunnies' (버니즈). However, 'Tokki' (토끼, the Korean word for rabbit) is frequently and affectionately used by the members and fans interchangeably, especially in Korean content and casual conversation. Both terms are recognized within the fandom culture.

### Q2: When was the NewJeans fandom name announced?

The official fandom name, 'Bunnies,' was announced on October 29, 2022, approximately three months after the group’s surprise debut release in July 2022. This timing allowed the group's core visual identity (the rabbit symbol) to be firmly established before the name was officially revealed.

### Q3: What is the name of the official NewJeans lightstick?

The NewJeans official lightstick is called the 'Binky Bong.' The design is directly related to the fandom name, featuring a stylized, minimalist rabbit head. The term 'Binky' is derived from the joyful, leaping dance a rabbit performs when happy, further reinforcing the playful connection between the group and their fans.
