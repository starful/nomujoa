In the vibrant and fast-paced world of K-POP, the relationship between an idol group and its fan base is foundational, often summarized in a single, powerful moniker: the official fandom name. For ZEROBASEONE (ZB1)—the nine-member group formed through Mnet’s competitive reality show *Boys Planet*—that name is ZEROSE (제로즈). More than just a title, ZEROSE is a deeply symbolic representation of the group’s identity and their commitment to their supporters.

As a K-POP Journalist and SEO specialist, understanding the etymology and cultural weight of terms like ZEROSE is crucial for both fan engagement and content visibility. This term is not merely decorative; it encapsulates the core philosophy of ZB1: the journey from 'zero' potential to 'one' unified completion. By thoroughly exploring the meaning of ZEROSE, we gain insight into the unique connection that drives one of the most successful fifth-generation K-POP groups. The name itself is an ingenious piece of branding, ensuring that the fans are intrinsically woven into the very fabric of the group’s existence, making the term highly searchable and emotionally resonant across global platforms.

## 2. History and Context

ZEROBASEONE, often abbreviated as ZB1, debuted in July 2023 under WAKEONE Entertainment. Their group name, ZEROBASEONE, signifies the members' starting point as nine trainees (Zero) who will become one complete, unified group (One). The transition from 'zero' to 'one' represents both their journey and their future potential.

The official fandom name, ZEROSE, was announced shortly after the group’s formation and holds an even more profound, almost poetic, meaning than the group name itself. ZEROSE is derived through a clever linguistic manipulation of the group’s full name.

The Etymological Breakdown:

If you take the full group name, ZEROBASEONE, and remove the two central letters—the 'B' and the 'A'—what remains spells out ZEROSE.

*   Z-E-R-O-(B)-A-S-E-O-N-E
*   Z-E-R-O-S-E

This construction symbolizes that the fans (ZEROSE) are the very foundation and context that allows the group (ZB1) to exist and connect. The 'B' and 'A' (which are removed to form ZEROSE) represent the concepts of "Base" or "Being," signifying that ZEROBASEONE's foundation (base) is incomplete without the inclusion and support of their fans. In essence, the group cannot complete the journey from Zero to One without their ZEROSE. This interconnectedness fosters an incredibly strong sense of loyalty and mutual dependency within the fandom.

Furthermore, the Korean pronunciation (제로즈, *jerojeu*) mirrors the softness and familiarity intended by the members, making it easy and affectionate to use in daily conversation and cheering. This focus on symbiotic completion is a recurring theme in ZB1's messaging and promotional materials, solidifying ZEROSE's role as the indispensable counterpart to the idols.
