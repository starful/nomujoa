## 1. Introduction

NCTzen (시즈니), derived from the Korean pronunciation of 'citizen' (시티즌 - 'si-ti-jeun'), is the official fandom name for the groundbreaking South Korean boy group NCT (Neo Culture Technology) and its multiple subunits. The name was officially announced on June 12, 2017, marking a significant moment in establishing the identity and community surrounding the group. NCTzen are considered integral to NCT's unique concept and their ever-expanding universe, acting as participants and witnesses to the group's growth and evolution.  The fandom name encapsulates the idea of belonging and shared ownership within the NCT project.

## 2. Origins and Meaning of the Name

The name NCTzen was chosen to reflect the core concept behind NCT: Neo Culture Technology.  NCT's concept centers around unlimited members and various subunits that promote in different cities around the world. The "Neo Culture" aspect embodies a forward-thinking, experimental approach to music and performance, constantly pushing boundaries and embracing new ideas.

The term "citizen" was carefully selected by SM Entertainment to symbolize the fans' role as inhabitants of the "NCT universe." This implied a close relationship where NCTzens are actively involved in the group's journey, from their debut and comebacks to their expansion into new markets and sub-units. It signifies a feeling of belonging and shared responsibility in nurturing NCT's growth. The Korean phonetic spelling of 'citizen' as '시티즌' was cleverly adapted to '시즈니,' giving the fandom name a unique and memorable quality. The meaning suggests that NCTzens are more than just passive observers; they are active contributors to NCT's narrative.

## 3. Pronunciation and Romanization

While the romanization is often written as "NCTzen," the correct Korean pronunciation is closer to "Si-jeu-ni" (시즈니). This pronunciation should be considered when communicating with Korean-speaking fans or referencing the fandom in Korean contexts. Different platforms and individuals might use slight variations in romanization, but "NCTzen" remains the most widely accepted and recognized form.

## 4. The Symbolism and Identity of NCTzens

Being an NCTzen represents a commitment to supporting NCT's diverse units and ever-changing lineup. It requires an understanding of the group's complex structure and a willingness to embrace the variety of musical styles and concepts they present. NCTzens often identify strongly with one or more of the sub-units (NCT U, NCT 127, NCT DREAM, WayV), while simultaneously supporting the group as a whole.  This often leads to discussions and even friendly rivalries within the fandom, further enriching the community experience.

The lightstick, often jokingly referred to as a "Meumwonbong" (무원봉) due to its unique and modern design, serves as a unifying symbol for NCTzens.  The lightstick changes colors to represent the different subunits during concerts, visually showcasing the interconnectedness of the entire NCT family. Beyond the official merchandise, NCTzens often express their identity through fan art, fan fiction, covers, and other creative content inspired by NCT's music and concepts.

## 5. Sub-Fandoms within the NCTzen Community

While "NCTzen" encompasses the entire fandom, smaller, more localized sub-fandoms have emerged based on individual sub-units and member biases. These sub-fandoms provide spaces for fans to connect more deeply with specific aspects of NCT that resonate with them. Examples include:

*   NCT U Stans: Focus on NCT U's rotational unit performances and diverse member combinations.
*   NCT 127 Stans: Dedicated to NCT 127, the Seoul-based unit with a strong international presence.
*   NCT DREAM Stans: Support NCT DREAM, originally envisioned as a teenage unit that has since evolved with its members.
*   WayV Stans (WayZenNi): While WayV technically has its own fandom name ("WayZenNi"), they are also considered part of the broader NCTzen community, especially as WayV is considered an NCT sub-unit operating in the Chinese market.
*   Individual Member Stans: Fans who primarily focus on supporting a particular member of NCT across all units and activities.

These sub-fandoms often collaborate on projects and support each other, reinforcing the overall sense of community within the NCTzen fandom.

## 6. Activities and Engagement of NCTzens

NCTzens are known for their active participation in various activities related to NCT's promotion and success.  These activities include:

*   Streaming Music:  Actively streaming NCT's songs on various music platforms to improve chart performance.
*   Voting in Music Shows:  Participating in online and SMS voting to help NCT win awards on Korean music shows.
*   Social Media Promotion:  Using hashtags and sharing content on social media platforms to increase NCT's visibility and engagement.
*   Fan Projects:  Organizing and participating in various fan-led projects, such as charity donations, advertisement campaigns, and support events for NCT members.
*   Attending Concerts and Events:  Attending NCT's concerts, fan meetings, and other events to show their support in person.
*   Creating Fan Content:  Producing and sharing fan art, fan fiction, covers, and other creative content inspired by NCT.
*   Engaging in Online Communities:  Participating in online forums, social media groups, and other online communities dedicated to NCT.

NCTzens are also known for their dedication to translating and subtitling NCT's content for international fans, ensuring that language barriers do not hinder global engagement.

## 7. Relationship with SM Entertainment

NCTzens maintain a complex relationship with SM Entertainment, NCT's agency. While appreciative of the opportunities provided to NCT, they are also vocal about their concerns regarding the group's management, promotion, and treatment. This includes advocating for fair distribution of lines and screen time, improved marketing strategies, and greater consideration for member well-being.  NCTzens often use social media and other platforms to communicate their concerns to SM Entertainment and push for positive changes.

## 8. Impact and Influence

NCTzens have played a significant role in NCT's growth and success. Their unwavering support has helped NCT achieve numerous milestones, including chart-topping albums, sold-out concerts, and global recognition. They are also credited with promoting NCT's unique concept and helping the group stand out in the competitive K-pop landscape.  The fandom's global reach and diverse demographics have contributed to NCT's worldwide appeal.

## 9. WayZenNi: A Special Case

As mentioned earlier, WayV, the Chinese-based unit of NCT, officially has its own fandom name: WayZenNi (威珍妮). This acknowledges WayV's specific focus on the Chinese market and their unique cultural identity.  However, many WayZenNis also identify as NCTzens, recognizing WayV's connection to the broader NCT universe. The relationship between WayZenNi and NCTzen reflects the complex structure of NCT and its commitment to reaching diverse audiences.

## 10. Challenges and Criticisms

Like any large fandom, the NCTzen community has faced its share of challenges and criticisms. These include:

*   Fandom Wars: Occasional conflicts with other fandoms due to competition or differing opinions.
*   Toxic Fan Behavior: Instances of obsessive or disrespectful behavior from some individuals within the fandom.
*   Internal Conflicts: Disagreements and conflicts within the fandom over member biases, subunit preferences, and management concerns.
*   Concerns about Line Distribution: Frequent debates about the fairness of line distribution among NCT members in different songs and units.

Despite these challenges, the majority of NCTzens are committed to creating a positive and supportive community for both NCT and each other.

## 11. The Future of NCTzen

The future of the NCTzen fandom is intertwined with the continued evolution of NCT. As NCT expands its reach and explores new musical territories, NCTzens will likely play an increasingly important role in shaping the group's identity and success.  The fandom is expected to continue to grow and diversify, embracing new members from different backgrounds and cultures. The commitment to supporting NCT's diverse units and unique concept will remain central to the NCTzen identity. The future also likely involves more active participation in addressing issues within the fandom and advocating for positive changes within SM Entertainment's management of NCT.
