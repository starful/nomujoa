## 1. Introduction

Carat (캐럿), often stylized as CARAT, is the official fandom name of the thirteen-member South Korean boy group SEVENTEEN (세븐틴). Officially announced on February 14, 2016, during SEVENTEEN's 'Like SEVENTEEN - Boys Wish' encore concert, the name represents the close and invaluable relationship between SEVENTEEN and their fans. The term "Carat" is a unit of measurement for the mass of diamonds and other precious stones, symbolizing the value and brilliance that fans bring to SEVENTEEN. It signifies that the fans are the reason why SEVENTEEN shines and are essential to their existence and success.

## 2. Etymology and Meaning

The name "Carat" is deeply intertwined with SEVENTEEN's concept and branding, which frequently incorporates diamond imagery and references. The connection stems from the idea that SEVENTEEN's members represent the raw material, and their fans, the Carats, are the ones who polish and give them value, allowing them to shine brightly like a diamond. More specifically, the "17" in SEVENTEEN is often interpreted as the 13 members + 3 units + 1 fandom, with the Carats representing the "1" that completes the group.

Beyond the literal meaning of diamond weight, "Carat" also symbolizes the deep love, unwavering support, and essential role that fans play in SEVENTEEN's journey. The name acts as a constant reminder that the group's achievements are directly linked to the dedication and commitment of their fans, creating a strong sense of unity and shared purpose.

## 4. Official Fandom Colors and Symbol

While SEVENTEEN does not have official fandom colors in the traditional sense, the colors Rose Quartz and Serenity, were the group's official colors and were often associated with the fandom and the group's branding for several years. The colors were specifically chosen as harmonious and visually appealing together, reflecting the complementary relationship between SEVENTEEN and their fans. However, the group has since moved away from actively utilizing official colors in their branding and promotions.

The diamond symbol is heavily incorporated into SEVENTEEN's logo and album designs, serving as a constant reminder of the significance of Carats in their journey. The visual representation of a diamond reinforces the idea of brilliance, preciousness, and the enduring strength of the connection between SEVENTEEN and their fandom.
