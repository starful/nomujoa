## 1. Introduction

In the vibrant ecosystem of K-pop, the relationship between an idol group and its fandom is paramount, often encapsulated in a uniquely meaningful official name. For Tomorrow X Together (TXT), the globally impactful five-member group under Big Hit Music, that powerful identifier is "MOA." Far more than just an acronym, MOA functions as a foundational cornerstone of TXT’s universe, defining the synergy, hopes, and shared future between the members—Soobin, Yeonjun, Beomgyu, Taehyun, and Hueningkai—and their devoted audience.

Understanding "MOA" is essential for anyone wishing to comprehend TXT’s unique narrative. In a genre where fandom names carry emotional weight and symbolic depth, MOA stands out for its double significance: a carefully constructed acronym in English and a crucial word in Korean. This duality perfectly reflects TXT's brand identity, which constantly bridges youthful innocence with complex narrative storytelling. As TXT continues to break records and redefine what a fourth-generation K-pop group can achieve, the name MOA serves as a constant reminder that their success is inextricably linked to the collective energy of their supporters.

## 2. History and Context

The official fandom name, MOA (모아), was unveiled on August 22, 2019, roughly five months after TXT’s highly anticipated debut in March of the same year. The name itself is a portmanteau representing the phrase "Moments of Always."

This meaning is intentionally designed to symbolize the eternal bond between the group and their fans. It signifies that TXT and their fans will be together "Always," sharing every significant "Moment" of their journey, both professional and personal.

However, the depth of MOA is fully realized when translated into Korean. The verb *모으다* (mo-eu-da) means "to gather," "to collect," or "to assemble." Thus, the name MOA carries a secondary, powerful meaning: the members of TXT gather all the moments shared with their fans, and conversely, the fans (MOA) gather together to complete TXT. The idea is that TXT and MOA are two necessary halves, collecting pieces (moments, dreams, memories) to complete a beautiful, shared mosaic.

This concept aligns perfectly with TXT's overarching lore, which often deals with themes of connection, friendship, and navigating the confusing paths of youth. From their earliest releases like "Crown" to their complex narrative trilogy, The Dream Chapter, the underlying message is that companionship and gathering strength are key to overcoming challenges. The MOA name solidifies the fandom's role not just as observers, but as active participants in the group’s overarching story.

TXT's lightstick, officially dubbed the "MOA Bong," features a design that is meant to embody the group's youthful energy and fantastical concept. At concerts, the ocean of synchronized MOA Bongs transforms the venue into a collective space, visually demonstrating the "gathering" aspect of the name. The lightstick itself acts as a symbol of the gathered moments and shared light between the group and the fans.

Charity and Global Impact: Reflecting the positivity and collective strength inherent in the MOA name, TXT’s fanbase is well-known for organizing large-scale philanthropic projects. These "MOA projects" often involve charity donations, environmental cleanup initiatives, or support for causes championed by the members. These organized efforts are a real-world manifestation of the name’s core principle: gathering resources and positive actions to make a meaningful difference. The synergy between TXT's vibrant, positive image and MOA's dedication to impactful projects strengthens their collective reputation globally.

## 5. FAQ

### Q1: What does MOA stand for, and what does it mean in Korean?

MOA is an acronym that stands for "Moments of Always," symbolizing the continuous, eternal bond between Tomorrow X Together and their fans. In Korean, the pronunciation (모아) directly relates to the verb *모으다* (mo-eu-da), which means "to gather" or "to collect." Both meanings stress the idea of the group and fans coming together to share every moment.

### Q2: Is MOA the original fandom name chosen by TXT?

No. Initially, TXT announced a fandom name called "Young One." However, it was quickly revealed that this name was already in use by the fanbase of another artist (Tiffany Young). Big Hit Music promptly retracted "Young One" and conducted further selection, officially announcing "MOA" as the definitive fandom name in August 2019.

### Q3: How do the TXT members usually refer to MOA in casual conversation?

The members use "MOA" universally in both formal and casual settings, often paired with affectionate suffixes. They might say "우리 모아" (Uri MOA, meaning "Our MOA") or address them directly as "모아 분들" (MOA deul/boon-deul, meaning "The MOA people") when speaking formally, showcasing a deep sense of shared identity and belonging.
