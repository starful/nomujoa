## 1. Introduction

MOA (모아) is the official fandom name for the South Korean boy group Tomorrow X Together (TXT). The name was announced on August 22, 2019, approximately five months after TXT's debut. MOA stands for "Moments Of Alwaysness," signifying the commitment of both the group and their fans to cherish every moment together and build a strong, lasting relationship. It represents the idea that TXT and MOA complete each other, with MOA gathering all the pieces (moments) of TXT to form a greater whole, and vice versa. Beyond simply being a name, MOA represents a profound connection and collaborative journey between TXT and their supporters.

## 2. Meaning and Symbolism

The choice of "MOA" is rich with meaning, embodying several key aspects of the relationship between TXT and their fans:

*   Moments Of Alwaysness: This highlights the importance of cherishing every moment, both big and small, shared between TXT and MOA. It speaks to the enduring nature of their bond and the promise of creating lasting memories together. The concept of "Alwaysness" implies a future-oriented perspective, suggesting a commitment to a long and meaningful journey together.

*   Completing Each Other: A core message embedded in the name is the idea of mutual completion. TXT and MOA are seen as two parts of a whole, each needing the other to fully realize their potential. MOA provides the support, encouragement, and love that allows TXT to grow and thrive, while TXT offers their music, performances, and connection to MOA, enriching their lives in return. This interconnectedness is a cornerstone of the TXT-MOA relationship.

*   Gathering Fragments: The Korean meaning of "moa" (모아), which translates to "gather" or "collect," further reinforces the idea of MOA gathering all the pieces, the moments, the experiences, and the dreams of TXT. This signifies the active role MOA plays in supporting and contributing to TXT's journey, acting as a unifying force.

*   A Symbolic Representation of Support: Choosing a name that encapsulates such deep meaning underscores the importance TXT places on its fanbase. It’s not just a label, but a representation of the active role MOA plays in TXT's success and personal growth.

## 3. Announcement and Reception

The announcement of the fandom name was highly anticipated and celebrated by fans worldwide. TXT teased the announcement in the days leading up to August 22nd, building anticipation and excitement. Upon the official reveal, the name was met with overwhelmingly positive reactions. Fans resonated with the meaning behind "MOA," appreciating the thoughtfulness and the emphasis on mutual support and connection.

The announcement was promoted across various platforms including:

*   TXT's official social media channels: Including Twitter, Instagram, and Facebook.
*   Weverse: HYBE's official fan community platform, where TXT actively interacts with fans.
*   Live Streams: TXT often incorporates fan name related content in their VLive and Weverse live streams.
*   Official Merchandise: The MOA name and related imagery are commonly featured on official TXT merchandise.
