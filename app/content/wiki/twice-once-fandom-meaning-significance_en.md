## 1. Introduction

In the highly competitive landscape of K-POP, the relationship between an artist and their fans is critical, often serving as the foundation for long-term global success. For the legendary girl group TWICE, managed by JYP Entertainment, this foundational relationship is encapsulated perfectly by their official fandom name: ONCE. Far more than just a catchy label, ONCE represents a philosophy of mutual commitment, trust, and shared history that has fueled TWICE's ascent to becoming one of the most successful acts in modern K-POP. Understanding the meaning of ONCE is essential to grasping the emotional core of TWICE’s music and identity, as the name dictates the group's perspective on their audience. This article delves into the origin, cultural impact, and enduring significance of the TWICE-ONCE bond.

## 2. History and Context

The name ONCE was officially announced shortly after TWICE’s debut in 2015, establishing an immediate connection with their rapidly growing fanbase. The core meaning behind the name is rooted in a beautifully simple promise: "If you love us even once, we will repay your love twice as much." (원스: ‘한 번’ 사랑하면, 두 배의 사랑으로 갚겠다는 약속).

This framing is profoundly strategic and emotionally resonant. Firstly, it uses a numerical relationship that mirrors the group's own name, creating an immediate and inseparable pairing. Secondly, it elevates the fan’s contribution (the “once”) by promising a greater, overwhelming return (the “twice”). This implies that the group’s existence and continued efforts are directly motivated by the fans’ initial faith and support.

This concept of reciprocity is foundational to TWICE's public persona. From early reality show appearances to their sold-out stadium tours years later, the members consistently emphasize that their achievements are joint victories shared with ONCE. This historical context ensures that every interaction, from fan sign events to album releases, is viewed through the lens of delivering on that original promise of returning love twofold. The longevity and stability of the TWICE-ONCE relationship stand as a testament to the power of this initial naming philosophy.

## 3. Real-world Examples and Fan Culture

The identity of the ONCE fandom is marked by loyalty, positivity, and a strong sense of unified support, often visible through specific cultural phenomena.

### The Candybong

The official lightstick, affectionately known as the Candybong, is a primary symbol of the ONCE identity. Its dual-sphere design and bright, changeable colors perfectly represent the playful, sweet image of TWICE. Crucially, the lightstick often becomes a symbol of unity in large concert venues, creating a "Candybong ocean" that visually reaffirms the collective power of the fandom and provides TWICE with a recognizable, supportive environment across the globe.

### Fan Projects and Dedication

ONCEs are globally renowned for sophisticated and extensive fan projects. During anniversaries or member birthdays, ONCEs routinely mobilize massive fundraising campaigns for charitable causes, placing advertisements in major cities worldwide, and organizing elaborate video tributes. This collective action highlights the commitment of the fandom not just to the group, but to reflecting TWICE's generally positive image back into the world.

A notable example of ONCE resilience came during periods of member hiatus or temporary crises (such as Jihyo's temporary injuries or Tzuyu’s 2016 controversy). In these moments, the fan community demonstrated unwavering, protective support, leveraging social media to shield the members and emphasize that the "once" commitment meant standing by the group unconditionally, ensuring they could return "twice" the love when ready. This dynamic proves that the fandom is not passive; they are active protectors of the group's well-being.

### Fan Chants (Fanchants)

K-POP concerts are defined by coordinated fanchants, and TWICE’s are particularly energetic. When ONCEs chant the members’ names—*Nayeon! Jeongyeon! Momo! Sana! Jihyo! Mina! Dahyun! Chaeyoung! Tzuyu! TWICE!*—in unison during key songs, it reinforces their collective identity and provides audible, immediate support, fulfilling the promise of the reciprocal relationship in real time.
