## 1. Introduction

ONCE (원스) is the official fandom name for the South Korean girl group TWICE (트와이스), formed by JYP Entertainment through the reality show *SIXTEEN*. Announced on November 3, 2015, the name "ONCE" carries a profound meaning, signifying the inseparable bond between the group and their fans. The moniker implies that TWICE can touch their fans "once" through their music and performances, and in return, the fans ("ONCE") will provide them with all the love and support they need. This reciprocal relationship is central to the ethos of TWICE and how they interact with their dedicated fanbase.

## 2. Origin and Meaning of the Name

The derivation of "ONCE" stems directly from TWICE's name. JYP Entertainment strategically chose a name that emphasized the symbiotic relationship between the group and their supporters. The idea is encapsulated in the phrase: "If you love us ONCE, we will repay your love with TWICE our love." This highlights the commitment of TWICE to always strive to deliver exceptional content and performances for their ONCEs. The simplicity and elegance of the name, along with its profound meaning, have resonated deeply with fans globally, making it one of the most recognizable fandom names in K-Pop.

## 3. ONCE's Role and Activities

ONCEs are known for their passionate dedication to TWICE. Their activities are wide-ranging and crucial to TWICE's success:

*   Streaming & Music Consumption: ONCEs actively stream TWICE's music on various platforms (Spotify, Apple Music, Melon, etc.) to boost their chart performance. They purchase digital and physical albums, contributing significantly to TWICE's sales figures and award show points.
*   Social Media Engagement: ONCEs are highly active on social media platforms like Twitter, Instagram, and YouTube. They use trending hashtags, create fan edits, share news and updates, and actively promote TWICE to a wider audience. This online presence is vital for maintaining TWICE's visibility and popularity.
*   Voting: ONCEs participate enthusiastically in voting campaigns for various awards shows and online polls. Their collective voting power often plays a decisive role in securing wins for TWICE. Platforms like MAMA (Mnet Asian Music Awards), AAA (Asia Artist Awards), and The Fact Music Awards often witness huge ONCE participation.
*   Supporting TWICE's Merchandise & Endorsements: ONCEs actively purchase TWICE's official merchandise, including albums, light sticks ("Candybong"), apparel, and other collectibles. They also support brands that TWICE endorses, increasing brand awareness and driving sales.
*   Attending Concerts & Fan Meetings: ONCEs from around the world travel to attend TWICE's concerts and fan meetings. They create elaborate fan projects, sing along loudly, and shower the members with love and support during these events. The energy and enthusiasm of ONCEs during live performances are a defining characteristic of TWICE's concerts.
*   Community Building: ONCEs have formed numerous online and offline communities where they connect, share their love for TWICE, and organize support activities. These communities provide a sense of belonging and foster a strong bond amongst fans.
*   Charity and Philanthropy: Inspired by TWICE's charitable endeavors, some ONCE groups organize philanthropic activities in TWICE's name, supporting causes such as environmental protection, disaster relief, and education.

## 4. The "Candybong" (Official Light Stick)

The official light stick for TWICE is called the "Candybong" (캔디봉). It’s a significant symbol for ONCEs, representing their connection to the group. The original Candybong (version 1) was designed to resemble a lollipop, referencing TWICE's debut song "Like OOH-AHH." Subsequent versions, such as the Candybong Z and Candybong Infinity, have incorporated more sophisticated designs and features, including customizable colors and remote control capabilities that allow for synchronized light shows during concerts. The Candybong is more than just a light stick; it’s a symbol of ONCE's unity and their shared love for TWICE.

## 5. ONCE's Cultural Impact

ONCE's impact extends beyond simply supporting TWICE's music. They have contributed to the wider K-Pop culture through their active participation in online communities, creation of fan content, and organization of events. Their global presence has helped spread TWICE's music and influence to new audiences. The fandom's dedication and passion have also influenced other K-Pop fandoms, setting a high standard for fan engagement and support. Furthermore, the organized fan projects orchestrated by ONCE during concerts are often cited as examples of K-Pop fandom creativity and dedication.

## 6. Challenges and Controversies

Like any large fandom, ONCE has faced its share of challenges and controversies.

*   Fandom Wars: ONCE has sometimes been involved in conflicts with other K-Pop fandoms. These "fandom wars" can arise from disagreements over chart rankings, awards, or perceived disrespect towards TWICE.
*   Sasaeng Fans: TWICE, like many popular K-Pop groups, has had to deal with *sasaeng* fans – overly obsessive and intrusive fans who engage in stalking and other harmful behaviors. While a small minority, *sasaeng* fans can cause significant distress to the members and the wider fandom.
*   Internal Disputes: Occasionally, disagreements can arise within the ONCE fandom itself, often relating to opinions about TWICE's music, styling, or promotion. These internal disputes, while sometimes heated, are usually resolved amicably.
*   Criticism Regarding Music or Concepts: TWICE's musical direction and concepts have sometimes been subject to criticism from both inside and outside the fandom. Differing opinions on the group's evolution are a natural part of fandom dynamics.

## 7. ONCE and TWICE: A Reciprocal Relationship

The relationship between ONCE and TWICE is one built on mutual respect and appreciation. TWICE frequently acknowledges ONCE's support in their speeches, performances, and social media posts. They often dedicate songs and performances to their fans, expressing their gratitude for the unwavering love and dedication. This constant communication and acknowledgment solidify the bond between the group and their fanbase. TWICE actively interacts with ONCE through fan meetings, live streams, and social media, fostering a sense of connection and intimacy. This symbiotic relationship is a key element of TWICE's success and popularity.

## 8. Conclusion

ONCE is more than just a fandom name; it's a symbol of the powerful connection between TWICE and their fans. Their unwavering support, dedication, and passion have played a crucial role in TWICE's global success. The name "ONCE" perfectly encapsulates the reciprocal relationship between the group and their supporters, a bond that continues to strengthen with each passing year. From streaming and voting to attending concerts and engaging in charitable activities, ONCEs are an integral part of the TWICE story. Their impact on K-Pop culture is undeniable, making ONCE one of the most influential and respected fandoms in the industry.
