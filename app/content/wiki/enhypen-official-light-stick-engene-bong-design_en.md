For ENHYPEN, the global sensation formed through the survival show *I-LAND*, their official light stick—aptly named the ENGENE Bong—serves as a crucial pillar in their narrative construction. Released in late 2020, the ENGENE Bong was designed not just to illuminate arenas, but to visually encapsulate the group's foundational concept of connection, destiny, and the seven members' shared journey. ## 2. History and Context of the ENGENE Bong

The design of the ENGENE Bong is meticulously aligned with ENHYPEN’s overarching themes of finding a path and crossing boundaries, often referenced in their cinematic music videos and album concepts. The light stick's core shape immediately draws attention to two primary symbolic elements: the heptagon and the compass.

The Heptagonal Center:
The light stick features a clear outer sphere encapsulating a centerpiece shaped as a heptagon (a seven-sided polygon). This shape directly represents the seven members of ENHYPEN—Heeseung, Jay, Jake, Sunghoon, Sunoo, Jungwon, and Ni-ki—signifying that they are stronger together, connected within a single entity. The heptagon, placed centrally within a circular globe, suggests completeness and infinity, mirroring the eternal nature of the bond they share with their fans.

The Compass and Dial Motif:
Perhaps the most distinctive feature is the compass-like dial surrounding the heptagon. This element plays directly into the group's debut theme of navigating an uncertain world and finding their direction. The compass symbolizes guiding ENHYPEN towards their goals and success, with ENGENE (the fandom name, meaning "engines" that help the group grow) acting as their crucial navigation system. The light stick is essentially a portable guide, symbolizing that wherever ENHYPEN goes, ENGENE is there to help chart the course.

The light stick utilizes Bluetooth technology, allowing it to sync wirelessly with concert central controls, creating the famous "ENHYPEN Ocean." The color scheme primarily focuses on variations of white and a deep, royal blue, invoking feelings of space, purity, and destiny—themes prevalent throughout their discography, especially tracks like "Drunk-Dazed" and "Given-Taken."

## 5. FAQ

### Q1: What does the name "ENGENE Bong" mean?

The light stick is named after the fandom, ENGENE. The fandom name is a portmanteau combining "Engine," suggesting that fans are the driving force that helps ENHYPEN grow and move forward, and "Gene," implying that ENGENE and ENHYPEN share the same DNA and are fundamentally connected. "Bong" is simply the common Korean term for 'stick' or 'rod,' thus ENGENE Bong means 'The ENGENE Light Stick.'

### Q2: Can the ENGENE Bong change colors?

Yes, the ENGENE Bong is highly versatile. It features multiple LED colors and modes. Crucially, during concerts, the light stick’s color is controlled wirelessly by the venue's central system via Bluetooth connectivity (after seat pairing through the official app), ensuring all sticks change color simultaneously to match the performance's theme and mood.

### Q3: Where can the official ENGENE Bong be purchased?

The official light stick is primarily sold through the Weverse Shop, which is the official global merchandise platform for ENHYPEN (under HYBE Corporation). It is also sometimes available through official partnership distributors or at concert venues during tour dates, though fans are always advised to purchase through Weverse to ensure authenticity and guaranteed concert pairing functionality.
