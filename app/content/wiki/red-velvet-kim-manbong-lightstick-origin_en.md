## 1. Introduction

In the vibrant world of K-pop, the official light stick (응원봉, *eungwonbong*) serves as more than just merchandise; it is a critical emblem of group identity and fan solidarity. For SM Entertainment’s global sensation, Red Velvet, their light stick holds a particularly special place in fan culture, thanks to its surprisingly simple design and the highly endearing, fan-given nickname: ‘Kim Manbong’ (김만봉).

Red Velvet is renowned for its concept duality—balancing the bright, quirky ‘Red’ side with the sophisticated, mature ‘Velvet’ side. While their music and visuals are often complex and thematic, their official cheering tool possesses an unexpectedly charming simplicity that perfectly captures the group’s whimsical spirit. Understanding the origin and evolution of the Kim Manbong is essential for anyone seeking to appreciate the deep, humorous bond between Red Velvet and their devoted fandom, ReVeluv.

## 2. History and Context

Red Velvet officially debuted in 2014, but they were notably one of the last major SM groups to release an official light stick. When the first official version was finally introduced in 2017, it needed to visually represent the group’s unique identity.

### The Official Design and Color Palette

The core design of the light stick features a clear sphere encasing the Red Velvet logo, which is often stylized as a stylized, crown-like R-V monogram. The handle is generally white. What truly distinguishes the light stick is its official color representation. Unlike groups that utilize a single, saturated color, Red Velvet’s official fandom colors are various shades of pastel, specifically pastel pink and coral. When illuminated, the Kim Manbong produces a soft, almost ethereal glow, reflecting the group’s dreamy ‘Velvet’ side while retaining the playful energy of their ‘Red’ side.

### The Origin of ‘Kim Manbong’

The term ‘Kim Manbong’ is a wholly fan-driven phenomenon. After the light stick’s release, ReVeluvs, known for their sharp wit and deep affection for the group, began to affectionately personify the cheering tool.

*   ‘Bong’ (봉): This is the standard Korean suffix for 'stick' or 'rod,' used in all light stick names.
*   ‘Kim Man’ (김만): This is the affectionate and nonsensical part of the nickname. While there are many urban myths about its exact origin, the most accepted theory is that fans began treating the stick like a slightly awkward, fifth member of the group, giving it a mundane, relatable Korean surname (Kim) combined with a simple, funny given name (Man). The stick's shape—often noted for its distinct, slightly chunky silhouette compared to the trend of sleek, minimalist light sticks—lent itself well to this friendly teasing.

Crucially, the nickname stuck because the members of Red Velvet themselves embraced it wholeheartedly. They frequently refer to the light stick as ‘Kim Manbong’ during concerts, livestreams, and fan calls, treating it as an actual entity. This validation transformed a simple fan nickname into an inseparable part of Red Velvet’s official iconography.

## 3. Real-world Examples and Fan Culture

The Kim Manbong is not just a piece of merchandise; it is a central character in the ReVeluv fandom narrative, frequently appearing in memes, fan art, and social media interactions.

### The Concert Ocean

During Red Velvet concerts, the synchronized glow of thousands of Kim Manbongs creates a uniquely soft, pastel ocean. While other K-pop oceans are known for vibrant, deep colors, the Red Velvet ocean is characterized by its soothing, unified glow, reflecting the sophisticated visual aesthetic the group often employs. The stick’s ability to change colors to match specific songs—from a fierce red for “Bad Boy” to a soft pink for “Psycho”—showcases its technical capabilities, but fans often focus on the humor of the object itself.

### Idol Interaction and Memes

The Kim Manbong is constantly featured in humorous fan content. Fans often create scenarios where the light stick is anthropomorphized, expressing feelings or engaging in antics. When members hold the light stick, they often treat it as a microphone or even a pet. This interaction generates immense SEO visibility; searches for "Red Velvet official merchandise" invariably lead users to content discussing the light stick's nickname, strengthening the brand identity. The stick symbolizes the unique connection RV has cultivated: sophisticated music delivered with a deeply personal, quirky charm.

## 5. FAQ

### Q1: What is the official name of Red Velvet’s light stick?

While almost universally referred to by the fan nickname ‘Kim Manbong,’ the official product is simply listed by SM Entertainment as the “Red Velvet Official Fanlight” or “Red Velvet Official Light Stick.” The nickname has effectively overshadowed the official product description in fan communities and media coverage.

### Q2: Why did fans name it ‘Kim Manbong’?

The name is a result of affectionate fan humor and the desire to personify the light stick. ‘Bong’ means stick, and ‘Kim Man’ is a simple, humorous, invented name. The nickname gained traction due to the light stick’s slightly distinctive shape, which fans found endearing and worthy of a personal identity, distinguishing it from other group light sticks.

### Q3: What is the significance of the light stick’s colors?

The Kim Manbong primarily illuminates in Red Velvet’s official pastel colors (pastel pink/coral/apricot). These soft hues reflect the group’s unique brand identity, which often utilizes subtle, dreamy, and sophisticated visual palettes rather than standard primary colors. The light stick is also capable of changing colors dynamically during concerts via central control, matching the theme and mood of specific performance stages.
