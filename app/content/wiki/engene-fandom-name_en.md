## 1. Introduction

ENGENE (엔진) is the official fandom name for the South Korean boy group ENHYPEN (엔하이픈). Officially announced on October 9, 2020, through a live broadcast, the name reflects the significant and symbiotic relationship between the group and their dedicated fanbase. ENGENE embodies a dual meaning, symbolizing the crucial role fans play in the group's success and trajectory, while simultaneously highlighting the collaborative spirit and shared identity that binds ENHYPEN and their supporters. ENGENE is more than just a name; it represents a dedicated community actively involved in supporting ENHYPEN's activities, achievements, and overall journey within the K-Pop landscape.

*   Engine: The first, and perhaps most straightforward, interpretation is that ENGENEs are the "engines" that power ENHYPEN. This encompasses everything from streaming their music and purchasing their albums to voting in music shows and promoting the group's activities online and offline. Without the dedication and passion of ENGENEs, ENHYPEN's journey would be significantly hindered.

The experiences, memories, and successes are shared and contribute to the ongoing narrative of both the group and the fandom. This emphasizes a sense of unity and collaboration, implying that they are intrinsically linked.

This dual meaning makes "ENGENE" a particularly apt and impactful fandom name, conveying a deep sense of responsibility, interdependence, and shared identity.
