## 1. Introduction
In the fast-evolving landscape of K-pop, the "center" position carries immense weight, acting as the visual and performance anchor of a group. Since RIIZE debuted under SM Entertainment, Wonbin has captured the attention of fans and industry experts alike. While many idols are praised for their looks, Wonbin represents a rare synergy of "unreal" visuals and technical mastery that redefines what it means to be a 5th-generation K-pop star. His presence is not merely about standing in the middle of a formation; it is about embodying the "emotional pop" genre that RIIZE has pioneered. As the group continues to dominate global charts, Wonbin's role as the face of the group has become a central topic of discussion, making him one of the most influential figures in modern music culture.

## 2. History and Context
SM Entertainment has a long-standing history of producing legendary "visual centers," from TVXQ's Jaejoong to EXO's Kai and NCT's Taeyong. Wonbin follows this prestigious lineage but brings a fresh, modern sensibility to the role. Before the group’s official debut in September 2023, Wonbin’s reveal caused an immediate sensation across social media platforms. His signature long hair during the "Get A Guitar" era broke traditional boy group styling norms and established a nostalgic yet trendy "90s heartthrob" aesthetic. 

Wonbin was not just chosen for his looks; his background as a skilled dancer and guitarist played a crucial role in his selection as the center. In K-pop, the center must lead the group’s performance energy, and Wonbin’s ability to execute complex choreography with fluid precision while maintaining perfect facial expressions has solidified his position. His journey from a mysterious trainee to the most talked-about rookie reflects SM’s strategic shift toward versatile idols who can bridge the gap between classic K-pop idolism and contemporary artistry.

## 3. Real-world Examples and Fan Culture
The impact of Wonbin’s visuals is best seen in his viral "fancams" and social media presence. During the promotions for "Get A Guitar" and "Talk Saxy," specific clips of Wonbin went viral for what fans call his "CG-like visuals"—referring to his appearance being so perfect it looks computer-generated. On platforms like X (formerly Twitter) and TikTok, the term "Wonbin is a genre" has gained traction, suggesting that his style and aura are unique enough to stand as their own category.

Fan culture surrounding Wonbin also emphasizes his "gap moe"—the contrast between his cold, charismatic appearance on stage and his shy, warm personality during variety shows and fan meetings. BRIIZE (the official fandom) frequently creates memes and digital edits that highlight his "cat-like" features, often comparing him to various feline characters. This deep engagement goes beyond just admiring his face; fans celebrate his growth as a musician, particularly when he performs live guitar solos during concerts, proving that his "center" status is backed by genuine talent.
